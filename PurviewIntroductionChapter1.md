# Chapter 1: The Business Problem
 
Organizations rarely suffer from a lack of data. They suffer from a lack of context and control.

People do not always know where sensitive information is stored. A file may be copied from SharePoint to a managed laptop, sent through email, uploaded to a cloud service, or included in an AI prompt. A database table may feed several reports, but nobody remembers which team owns it. Legal teams may need information preserved immediately. Compliance teams may need evidence that a control is operating. Security teams may need to understand whether an unusual download was accidental or part of a broader pattern.

These are related questions:

-	What data do we have?
-	What does it mean?
-	Who can access it?
-	How is it being used?
-	How should it be protected?
-	How long should it be kept?
-	What happened when something went wrong?

Purview connects capabilities that address these questions. It does not remove the need for policy, ownership, legal interpretation, or human judgment. Instead, it provides shared signals, controls, workflows, and evidence so different teams can work from a more consistent understanding of the data estate. DSPM, for example, brings together insights from information protection, DLP, Insider Risk Management, and investigations to answer practical questions about where data resides, who can access it, and whether it is protected.

A helpful way to frame the challenge is to separate visibility from control. An organization first needs a shared picture of its important information, then clear decisions about ownership, access, protection, retention, and investigation. Purview supports that sequence so teams can address the same business problem from different professional perspectives without working in isolation.

For example, a project team may store customer contracts in several places. Sales knows the customers. Legal knows the obligations. Security sees sharing activity. Purview helps these teams build one view of the same information and agree on the right controls.
